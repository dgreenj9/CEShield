# The CE Shield voice

The QOVES register, translated from Alodaptive to credential intelligence
(2026-08-25, founder-directed). This doc is the filter every member-facing
and public sentence passes through. The parent doctrine lives at
`~/projects/allostatic/docs/design/voice.md`; this is its application here.

True north: findings read as observation, measurement, comparison, flat.
Marketing claims are countable capabilities, never vibes and never promises.
The artifact (the ranked field, a dossier's citation list, the Ledger's
computed hours) does the persuading; copy labels it.

## The laws

1. **The artifact carries the argument.** The scores, the citations, and
   the computed hours persuade. If the surface shows it, no sentence
   explains it. Cut copy before improving it.
2. **Claims are countable and true.** 143 credentials. 8,609 citations.
   Five lenses. Three states. Librarian-reviewed. Nothing the product does
   not verifiably do, no invented cadence, no outcome promises, and no
   claims about competitors we cannot count ("most platforms miss this"
   is banned until we can name the platforms and the miss).
3. **Compliance copy is observation → benchmark → translation.** What was
   logged, against the state rule, and what remains: "12 of 40 hours.
   Ethics 0 of 3. 240 days to renewal." The rule citation is the
   benchmark; always name it.
4. **Precise professional vocabulary, plainly used.** CE cycle, mandatory
   category, format cap, jurisprudence, evidence tier. Glossed on first
   use in long-form, never dumbed down in labels.
5. **Self-names.** The field (the ranked instrument), a dossier (a
   credential's evidence page), the Ledger (compliance), claims (your
   self-reported credentials), the binder (exports). Never "matrix" in
   copy, never "platform", never "OS", never "solution".
6. **Short.** Headlines ≤ 6 words. Body lines earn every word. Second
   person sparingly, flat.
7. **No em dashes anywhere** in rendered copy, titles, or metadata.
   Use `·`, a comma, a colon, or a new sentence.

## Banned register (both marketing and product)

Exclamation points. Cheerleading. Metaphor and personification (claims
that "cite themselves", a CV that "lives", scores that "know"). The
self-praise words: honest, honestly, actually, quietly, simply,
seamlessly. Superlatives we cannot count. Outcome promises ("protect your
license" as a guarantee); capabilities only.

## Load-bearing lines (keep verbatim)

These are trust currency, already in the register:

- "$0 from the programs we score. No ads. No sponsored rankings."
- "Scores are never sponsored."
- "Free for students, always."
- "Self-reported." (on every claims surface)
- The methodology limitations section: limits stated up front are a
  feature, not a concession.

## Credibility construction

Countable inputs stated plainly, named methods, limitations up front,
human review named ("curated with research librarians"). One retained
statistic per claim. Myth-busting is trust currency: say what we do not
do (ads, sponsorship, selling data) as flatly as what we do.
